I am a postdoctoral researcher at CASA (Centre for Analysis, Scientific Computing and Applications) at Eindhoven University of Technology. I completed my PhD in Physical and Theoretical Chemistry at the University of Rennes. I use computational tools to describe molecular and solid-state systems. I focus in particular on applying machine learning to improve the computational modelling of materials, making simulations faster, more accurate, and more widely applicable.
