I was born and raised in Greece where I studied Physics in the University of Athens. After the completion of my military service, I moved to the Netherlands for my M.Sc. in Theoretical Physics in the Utrecht University, and later to the University of Amsterdam as a doctoral candidate. I joined the TU/e as lecturer in the Department of Mathematics in 2019.
